# Restaurant Analytics Business Definitions

## Net Sales

**Business Owner:** Finance

**Definition:**

Net Sales = Gross Sales - Refunds - Discounts

### Included

- Completed orders

### Excluded

- Sales tax
- Tips
- Voided transactions

### Questions

- Should gift cards count as revenue when sold or when redeemed?
- Should partial refunds reduce Net Sales?
- How are employee meals handled?
